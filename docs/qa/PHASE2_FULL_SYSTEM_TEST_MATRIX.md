## Priority rules

- **P0/Critical:** unauthorized cross-household access/mutation, unreconciled balances, data loss, duplicate financial posting.
- **P1/High:** broken core workflow, incorrect period/financial summary, unsafe financial validation, unavailable operational recovery.
- **P2/Medium:** UI, accessibility, reporting/export or performance issue without immediate ledger/security impact.

Budget rollover, refund classification, source effective dates, and recurrence calendars affect financial results. They remain blocked until product rules are approved; QA must not invent those rules.
